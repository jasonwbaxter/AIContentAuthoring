## Definitions and Methodology

### CPI Calculation

Dolor sit amet, consectetur adipiscing elit, sed do eiusmod tempor incididunt ut labore et dolore magna aliqua. The CPI is calculated using:

- **Base Period**: January 2020 = 100
- **Basket**: 80,000 products and services
- **Survey**: 10,000 retail outlets monthly
- **Formula**: Laspeyres price index
- **Frequency**: Monthly, released monthly

### GDP Calculation

Sed do eiusmod tempor incididunt ut labore et dolore magna aliqua. GDP is measured using three approaches:

1. **Production Approach**: Sum of value-added
2. **Income Approach**: Sum of incomes
3. **Expenditure Approach**: Consumption + Investment + Exports - Imports
