# Monetary Policy

Monetary policy is the set of actions used by the central bank to keep inflation low and stable while supporting sustainable economic activity.

## Policy Goal

Our primary objective is medium-term price stability. Stable inflation supports planning, investment, and employment outcomes.

## Main Tools

- Policy rate decisions
- Open market operations
- Liquidity facilities
- Forward-looking communication

## Decision Cadence

The policy committee meets on a published calendar. Each meeting evaluates inflation trends, growth conditions, labor-market data, exchange-rate dynamics, and external risks.

## Communication

After each meeting we publish:

- Decision statement
- Vote distribution
- Rationale and risk assessment
- Updated macroeconomic outlook
