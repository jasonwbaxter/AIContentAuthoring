# Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how Contoso Bank uses cookies and similar technologies on this website.

## What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files stored in your browser to support core site functions, preferences, and analytics.

## Cookie Categories

- Strictly necessary cookies: required for security and core navigation.
- Analytics cookies: help us understand site usage patterns.
- Preference cookies: remember choices such as language settings.

## Consent and Control

- Non-essential cookies are optional and can be disabled.
- You can manage cookie settings through the site preference tool and browser controls.
- Disabling some cookies may affect certain features.

## Retention

Cookie lifespan varies by purpose. Session cookies expire when you close your browser, while persistent cookies remain for a defined period.
