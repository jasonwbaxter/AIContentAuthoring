# Quarterly Bulletin — Q4 2025

## What You Need to Know

Q4 2025 brought some good news about inflation—it's starting to come down. However, prices for everyday items like services are still climbing faster than the Contoso Bank would like. Here's what happened and what it means for you.

## Key Numbers for Your Wallet

- **Inflation is cooling down:** Prices rose 5.1% year-over-year (that's better than earlier)
- **Core inflation (excluding food and energy):** Still at 4.6% 
- **Your income:** Real wages continued to grow, helping you keep up with rising costs
- **Unemployment:** Stayed relatively steady around 6.3%
- **Borrowing costs (Repo rate):** Held at 8.25%

> These are illustrative figures for demonstration purposes.

## What's Happening in the Economy

The global economy slowed down in Q4, but locally, people kept spending thanks to steady wage growth and job security. Businesses invested unevenly, with some sectors doing better than others.

### Why Are Prices Still Rising?

Inflation eased off, mainly because energy prices dropped and we had favorable base effects from last year. However, services—things like healthcare, education, dining out—are still getting more expensive. Companies are passing along higher wages to customers through price increases.

### Your Job and Income

The job market remained fairly stable. Unemployment crept up slightly, but more people entered the workforce. Wage growth continued, which helped workers keep pace with inflation, though not always completely.

## What's Happening in Financial Markets

Banks and financial institutions have plenty of money available. Interest rates in the money market track the official repo rate. Government bonds became slightly less expensive (good news if you own them) as investors felt more confident.

## What the Monetary Authority Is Doing

The Contoso Bank decided to keep interest rates unchanged at 8.25%. They want to see the effects of previous rate hikes settle in before making more changes. They're balancing two goals:
- Getting inflation down to where it should be
- Making sure the economy can grow

**What to watch for:**
- Unpredictable global demand could cause problems
- Supply shocks (disruptions in production) could push prices up
- The Contoso Bank will keep a close eye on service prices

## Quick Summary of Key Data

| What | Last Year (Q4 2024) | Last Quarter (Q3 2025) | Now (Q4 2025) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Overall inflation | 6.0% | 5.6% | 5.1% |
| Core inflation (without food/energy) | 5.2% | 4.9% | 4.6% |
| Economic growth | 2.8% | 1.5% | 1.2% |
| Unemployment | 6.8% | 6.1% | 6.3% |
| Borrowing rate (Repo) | 7.75% | 8.25% | 8.25% |

## What This Means for You

**Good news:** Inflation is coming down, and your wages are keeping pace.

**Things to watch:** Core inflation is still above target, and global uncertainty could affect your job or spending.

**Next steps:** The Contoso Bank will continue monitoring prices and may adjust rates if needed.
