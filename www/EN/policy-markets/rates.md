# Interest Rates

Interest-rate policy is the main channel used to steer inflation toward target while considering broader economic conditions.

## Rate Framework

- Policy benchmark rate announced after committee meetings
- Supporting market rates monitored daily
- Transmission indicators tracked across credit and deposit products

## Decision Factors

- Expected inflation versus target
- Demand and output conditions
- Wage and cost pressures
- External and financial risk conditions

## Reading a Rate Decision

Each decision should be interpreted alongside the published risk assessment, inflation outlook, and implementation guidance.
