### Key Responsibilities

- **Price Stability**: Maintain inflation within the target range through various monetary policy tools
- **Financial System Stability**: Oversee and regulate the banking system to ensure soundness
- **Currency Management**: Manage the national currency and foreign exchange reserves
- **Payment Systems**: Ensure efficient functioning of the payments and settlement systems

- Support efficient price discovery in financial markets
