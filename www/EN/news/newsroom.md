# Newsroom

The newsroom is the primary source for official Contoso Bank updates.

## Release Types

- Policy statements
- Statistical releases
- Research and working papers
- Event notices and speaking engagements

## Release Governance

- Content passes legal and policy checks before publication.
- Market-sensitive material follows strict embargo controls.
- Corrections are published transparently with timestamped updates.

## Media Requests

Journalists can submit questions through the media desk. Requests should include deadline, publication, and topic scope to support timely response.

## Operating Hours

- Standard responses: business days, 08:00-17:00
- High-priority events: extended coverage windows as announced
