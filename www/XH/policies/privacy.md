# Privacy Policy

Contoso Bank is committed to responsible handling of personal information in line with applicable privacy and data-protection requirements.

## Information We Collect

- Contact details provided in forms
- Technical data such as browser type, device, and usage logs
- Communication records for support and service quality

## Why We Process Personal Information

- To deliver and secure digital services
- To respond to user requests and statutory obligations
- To improve website functionality and accessibility

## Legal Basis and Safeguards

Processing is limited to legitimate, specified purposes and protected through access controls, encryption, retention limits, and incident-response procedures.

## Your Rights

Subject to law, you may request access, correction, deletion, objection, or restriction of processing for your personal information.

## Contact

Privacy requests can be directed to the designated information office using the contact details on the Help and Contact section.
