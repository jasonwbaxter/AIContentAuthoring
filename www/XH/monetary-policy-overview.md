### Uxanduva Oluphambili

- **Uzinzo lwamaxabiso**: Gcina ukunyuka kwamaxabiso kuluhlu ekujoliswe kulo usebenzisa izixhobo ezahlukeneyo zomgaqo-nkqubo wemali
- **Uzinzo lwenkqubo yezemali**: Jongamela kwaye ulawule inkqubo yebhanki ukuqinisekisa ukomelela
- **Ulawulo lwemali**: Lawula imali yesizwe kunye neendawo zokugcina zotshintshiselwano lwangaphandle
- **Iinkqubo zokuhlawula**: Qinisekisa ukusebenza kakuhle kweenkqubo zokuhlawula nokuhlaliswa
