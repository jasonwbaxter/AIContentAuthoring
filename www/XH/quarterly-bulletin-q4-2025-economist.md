# IBhulethini yeKota - Q4 2025

## Isishwankathelo soLawulo

I-Q4 2025 ibone ukuthomalala kokunyuka kwamaxabiso e-CPI ukuya ku-5.1% (y/y) kunye nomsebenzi oqinileyo kumacandelo aphambili oqoqosho, nangona iimeko zokukhula kwehlabathi ziye zaba buthathaka. IGunya loMgaqo-nkqubo weMali ligcine imeko yomgaqo-nkqubo kwi-825 basis points kuyo yonke ikota, lisithi i-core inflation ihleli iqinile kunye nokungaqiniseki okuphezulu kwicala langaphandle. Le bhulethini inika uvavanyo olubanzi lotshintsho lwe-macro, iimeko zeemarike zezimali, kunye nemiba yomgaqo-nkqubo kwikota yokugqibela ka-2025.

## Amanqaku Aphambili

- **I-Headline CPI (y/y):** 5.1% (Q4 2025, okwethutyana)
- **I-Core CPI (y/y):** 4.6% (ikhupha ukutya namandla, ingaphezulu kwe-4.5% midpoint ekujoliswe kuyo)
- **I-Real GDP (q/q, annualised):** 1.2% (Q4 2025, okwethutyana) - yehle ukusuka ku-1.5% kwi-Q3
- **Inqanaba lokungaphangeli:** 6.3% (Q4 2025) - linyuke ukusuka ku-6.1% kwi-Q3
- **I-Repo rate (ekupheleni kwexesha):** 825 bps (igcinwe kuyo yonke ikota)
- **Ukuthatha inxaxheba kwabasebenzi:** ukuphucuka okuncinci emaphethelweni

> Qaphela: Amanani yidatha yesampulu okwethutyana ngeenjongo zokubonisa. Ukukhutshwa okusemthethweni kusalindwe.

## Umxholo woQoqosho

Ukukhula kwehlabathi kuye kwaba buthathaka ngexesha lekota, kubonisa imfuno yangaphandle ebuthathaka kumaqabane aphambili orhwebo kunye neemeko zezimali zehlabathi eziqinileyo. I-manufacturing PMI ihleli ingaphantsi kwama-50 kumazwe amaninzi aphuhlileyo. Ekhaya, ukomelela kwenkcitho yabathengi kuxhaswe kukukhula kwemivuzo kunye nokuzinza kwemarike yemisebenzi, ngelixa iinjongo zotyalo-mali zithambile.

### Amaxabiso noDynamics yoNyuko lwaMaxabiso

Ukuthomalala kwe-headline inflation ukusuka ku-5.6% (Q3 2025) ukuya ku-5.1% (Q4 2025) kuqhutywe ikakhulu zezi:

1. **I-base effects** ukusuka kutshintsho olukhulu lwamaxabiso amandla kwi-Q4 2024
2. **Ukwehla kwamaxabiso amandla** emva kokuphinda kuhlengahlengiswe ixabiso leoli yehlabathi
3. **Ukuthomalala kokunyuka kwamaxabiso okutya** ukuya kumanqanaba aphakathi

Nangona kunjalo, ukuqina kwe-core inflation kwi-4.6% kubonisa:

- **Uxinzelelo lwamaxabiso eenkonzo:** ngakumbi kwezempilo, kwimfundo, nakwizindlu zokuhlala
- **I-wage-price dynamics:** ukudluliselwa kweendleko zabasebenzi kubavelisi beenkonzo
- **Uhlengahlengiso lwamaxabiso alawulwayo:** iitarifu zamanzi nombane kunye nezothutho
- **Amandla okubeka amaxabiso kumacandelo:** angalinganiyo phakathi kweempahla neenkonzo

### Dynamics zeMarike yeMisebenzi

Imarike yemisebenzi ibonise isantya esixubeneyo:

- **Ukungaphangeli kunyuke** ukusuka ku-6.1% ukuya ku-6.3%, kubonisa ukuthomalala kwemfuno yabasebenzi
- **Ukuthatha inxaxheba kwabasebenzi kuphucuke kancinane**, kubonisa iintshukumo zentsebenziswano ezichasene nomjikelo
- **Ukukhula kwemivuzo okuzinzileyo** kuhlale kumanqanaba aphezulu, kugcina uxinzelelo lonyuko lwamaxabiso
- **Umahluko phakathi kwamacandelo:** ukukhula kwemisebenzi kwiinkonzo kudlule kumacandelo avelisa iimpahla

Ubudlelwane be-Phillips curve busabonakala, nangona kukho iinguqu zesakhiwo ezinxulumene neemeko ze-demography kunye nokungakhululeki kwemarike yemisebenzi.

## Iimarike zeMali neeMeko zoLwelo

### Iimarike zeMali

Amanqanaba e-interbank overnight athengiselane kufutshane ne-repo rate ye-825 bps, nto leyo ebonisa ulindelo lomgaqo-nkqubo oluqinileyo. Imisebenzi ye-corridor igcine ukusebenza kakuhle ngemfudlana encinci yotshintsho. Ukusetyenziswa kwe-standing facility kuhlale kusezantsi, kubonisa ulwelo olwaneleyo kwimarike.

### Imarike yeZibambiso zikaRhulumente

I-yield curve ibonise ukuthamba kwicala elide, nezivuno zeminyaka eli-10 zehla malunga ne-40 basis points q/q. Olu tshintsho lubonisa:

- ukuphucuka kovakalelo lomngcipheko lwehlabathi emva kokungaqiniseki kwe-Q3
- ulindelo lokuba umgaqo-nkqubo wokuxhasa uza kuhlala ixesha elide kwihlabathi
- ukuhamba kwemali okuya kwizibambiso zikarhulumente kwiimarike ezisakhulayo

### Iimeko zeMarike yeTyala

Ukukhula kwetyala leebhanki kuthomalale ukuya ku-4.2% (y/y) ukusuka ku-4.8% kwi-Q3 2025, kubonisa imfuno ebuthathaka kwiindidi zotyalo-mali, ngelixa ityala labathengi lihlale lizinzile. I-NPL ratio ihleli kwi-3.1%.

## Amanyathelo oMgaqo-nkqubo weMali kunye noKhokelo oluJonge Phambili

IGunya loMgaqo-nkqubo weMali ligcine inqanaba lomgaqo-nkqubo kwi-825 basis points ngexesha le-Q4 2025, lisithi:

1. **Iimpembelelo zokudluliselwa ezilibazisekileyo** zokuqiniswa kwangaphambili zisafuna uvavanyo
2. **Ukuqina kwe-core inflation** ngaphezulu koluhlu ekujoliswe kulo
3. **Ukungaqiniseki kwangaphandle** kufuna indlela enobulumko
4. **Uzinzo lwezimali** kunye neemeko zetyala

### Uvavanyo lweSimo soMgaqo-nkqubo

Isimo esingathathi cala sibonisa uvavanyo olulinganiselweyo:

| Umba | Uvavanyo | Isalathiso |
|---|---|---|
| Isantya se-inflation | Siyathomalala kodwa i-core iqinile | Kancinci i-hawkish |
| Dynamics zokukhula | Ziyathamba, zingaphantsi komkhondo | Dovish |
| Iimeko zezimali | Ziya ziqina kwihlabathi | Dovish |
| Imarike yemisebenzi | Iyaphola isuka kwinqanaba eliphantsi | Neutral |

### Ukhokelo oluJonge Phambili

I-MPC iza kuqhubeka ihlalutya idatha ngexesha lokwenyani, igxile kwezi:

- umkhondo we-core inflation kunye namandla amaxabiso eenkonzo
- izalathisi zemfuno yangaphandle kunye namaxabiso ezinto ezithengiswayo
- izalathisi zozinzo lwezimali zasekhaya
- izalathisi zemisebenzi ezifika ngexesha lokwenyani

Utshintsho kumgaqo-nkqubo luya kuxhomekeka kwidatha kwaye lunxityiswe ngonxibelelwano olucacileyo kwangaphambili.

## Uvavanyo lweMngcipheko

### Imingcipheko ePhezulu kuNyuko lwaMaxabiso

1. **Ukothuka kwicala lonikezelo:** uxinzelelo lwe-geopolitics oluchaphazela amandla nokutya
2. **I-wage-price spirals:** ukukhula kwemivuzo okuphezulu okuqhubekisa i-inflation yeenkonzo
3. **Ummangaliso kwimfuno yehlabathi:** ukujika komkhwa wokuncipha kwe-inflation kumaqabane orhwebo
4. **Ukwehla kwexabiso lemali:** i-pass-through kwiindleko zokungenisa impahla

### Imingcipheko ePhantsi kuKhula

1. **Ukuba buthathaka kwemfuno yangaphandle:** ukudodobala kumaqabane aphambili orhwebo
2. **Ukuqina kweemeko zezimali:** ukuphuma kwemali kunye nokunyuka kwe-risk premia
3. **Ukuncipha kwetyala lasekhaya:** ukuba iimeko zezimali ziqina ngokukhawuleza
4. **Iimpembelelo zokuthembela:** ukungaqiniseki ngomgaqo-nkqubo kunciphisa utyalo-mali

## Isihlomelo: Amanqaku obuGcisa

### Imithombo yeDatha neendlela

- **Idatha ye-CPI:** iNational Statistics Authority, ukukhutshwa kwenyanga kunye nohlengahlengiso lwamaxesha onyaka nge-X-13ARIMA-SEATS
- **Iingqikelelo ze-GDP:** indlela ye-flash estimate ehambelana nemigangatho ye-SNA 2008
- **Idatha yemisebenzi:** iQuarterly Labour Force Survey enobunzima babemi obuhlengahlengisiweyo
- **Amanqanaba emarike yemali:** amanqanaba esalathiso abalwa njenge-volume-weighted daily average kwiintengiselwano ze-interbank

### Uhlengahlengiso lwamaxesha onyaka

Zonke iiseti zedatha, ngaphandle kwenqanaba lokungaphangeli, zihlelwe ngamaxesha onyaka. Uhlengahlengiso lwe-CPI lubonisa iipateni zexesha kwicandelo lokutya namandla.

### Isimo sokuThutyana

Amanani e-Q4 2025 asesisimo sokwethutyana de kugqitywe ukufakwa kwedatha ngamagunya ezibalo. Uhlaziyo lulindeleke kwezi ndawo:

- I-GDP (ukukhutshwa kukaFebruwari 2026)
- Idatha yemisebenzi (inani lokugqibela ngoMeyi 2026)
- Iinxalenye ezineenkcukacha ze-CPI (ukuqinisekiswa kwedatha yokuqala)
