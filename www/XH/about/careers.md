# Careers

Contoso Bank offers mission-driven careers for people who want to contribute to macroeconomic stability, financial-system resilience, and public service.

## Why Work Here

- Public-impact work with measurable outcomes.
- Exposure to policy, markets, technology, and data science.
- Structured learning pathways and mentorship.
- Inclusive, multidisciplinary teams.

## Career Areas

- Monetary economics and forecasting
- Financial markets and reserves operations
- Data engineering and statistical systems
- Cybersecurity and technology risk
- Communications, legal, and corporate services

## Recruitment Process

1. Online application and role matching.
2. Technical and behavioral assessments.
3. Panel interview and reference checks.
4. Offer, onboarding, and probation support.

## Early-Career Programs

- Graduate trainee intake (annual)
- Internship program (winter and summer cohorts)
- Apprenticeships in digital and operations disciplines

## Equal Opportunity

We recruit on merit and are committed to fair, transparent hiring practices.
