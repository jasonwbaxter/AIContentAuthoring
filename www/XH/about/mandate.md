# Mandate and Role

Contoso Bank's core objective is to maintain low and stable inflation over the medium term. Stable prices help households plan, businesses invest, and financial markets allocate capital efficiently.

## Primary Objective

- Preserve purchasing power through credible inflation control.

## Supporting Objectives

- Contribute to financial stability by monitoring system-wide risks.
- Support sound functioning of money, bond, and foreign-exchange markets.
- Promote safe, efficient, and inclusive payment and settlement arrangements.

## Why Central Bank Independence Matters

International evidence shows that independent central banks are better placed to anchor inflation expectations and reduce long-run inflation volatility. Independence is paired with strong transparency requirements so the public can evaluate performance.

## How the Mandate Is Delivered

1. The policy committee sets the policy rate on a published schedule.
2. Operations teams implement that stance in market-facing facilities.
3. Analysts monitor inflation, growth, labor, and financial conditions.
4. Public communications explain decisions, risks, and outlook changes.

## Accountability and Reporting

- Monetary Policy Report (quarterly)
- Decision statement and vote summary (each meeting)
- Annual report and audited financial statements
