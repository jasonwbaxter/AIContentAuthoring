# Leadership and Governance

Contoso Bank is governed through a board-and-committee structure designed to balance technical rigor, institutional independence, and public accountability.

## Governance Structure

- Governor and Deputy Governors: Executive leadership and policy stewardship.
- Monetary Policy Committee (MPC): Decides the policy rate and policy stance.
- Risk and Audit Committee: Oversees internal controls, risk, and assurance.
- Ethics and Conduct Panel: Monitors integrity standards and conflict controls.

## Decision-Making Principles

- Evidence-based: Decisions are informed by data, models, and scenario analysis.
- Deliberative: Members review multiple policy options before voting.
- Transparent: Outcomes, rationale, and risk balance are published.

## Governance Safeguards

- Fit-and-proper standards for senior appointments.
- Mandatory declarations of financial interests.
- Segregation of policy decisions and operational execution.
- Periodic external and internal audits.

## Public Oversight

The bank appears before oversight bodies and publishes regular disclosures to ensure that independence is paired with accountability. Governance reports include attendance, key resolutions, and follow-up actions.
