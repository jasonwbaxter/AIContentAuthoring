# Policies and Compliance

This section summarises the internal standards Contoso Bank uses to protect trust, manage risk, and comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

## Policy Domains

- Information security and cyber resilience
- Data governance and records management
- Procurement integrity and vendor due diligence
- Market conduct and insider-information controls
- Business continuity and crisis management

## Compliance Framework

Our three-lines model supports effective control execution:

1. Business units own operational controls.
2. Risk and compliance teams set standards and monitor adherence.
3. Internal audit provides independent assurance.

## Breach and Incident Management

- Material incidents are escalated under defined severity levels.
- Root-cause analysis and remediation plans are mandatory.
- High-severity events are reported to relevant oversight structures.

## Training and Certification

All employees complete annual training on conduct, privacy, anti-fraud, and information security. Staff in high-risk functions complete additional role-specific certification.
