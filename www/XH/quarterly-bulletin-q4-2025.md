# IBhulethini yeKota - Q4 2025

## Isishwankathelo soLawulo

I-Q4 2025 ibonise ukuthomalala kokunyuka kwamaxabiso e-CPI ngelixa umsebenzi woqoqosho uqhubekile kwiicandelo eziphambili. IGunya loMgaqo-nkqubo weMali ligcine imeko yomgaqo-nkqubo ingatshintshanga kuyo yonke ikota, lisithi i-core inflation isahleli iphezulu kwaye iimeko zangaphandle azizinzanga. Le bhulethini inika isishwankathelo sotshintsho lwe-macro, iimeko zeemarike zezimali, kunye nemiba yomgaqo-nkqubo ekupheleni kuka-2025.

## Amanqaku Aphambili

- I-Headline CPI (y/y): 5.1% (Q4 2025, inani lesampulu)
- I-Core inflation (y/y): 4.6% (ikhupha ukutya namandla)
- I-Real GDP (q/q, annualised): 1.2% (Q4 2025, okwethutyana)
- Inqanaba lokungaphangeli: 6.3% (Q4 2025)
- I-Repo rate: 8.25% (inqanaba lomgaqo-nkqubo ekupheleni kwekota)

> Qaphela: Amanani angentla ngumxholo wesampulu wokubonisa.

## Umxholo woQoqosho

Ukukhula kwehlabathi kuthambe ngexesha lekota, kubonisa imfuno yangaphandle ebuthathaka kunye neemeko zezimali zehlabathi eziqinileyo. Ekhaya, inkcitho yabathengi ixhaswe kukukhula kwemivuzo kunye nokuqina kwemarike yemisebenzi, ngelixa utyalo-mali belungalingani phakathi kwamacandelo.

### Amaxabiso noNyuko lwaMaxabiso

Ukunyuka kwamaxabiso kuthomalale kwi-Q4, ngenxalenye ngenxa yamaxabiso asezantsi wamandla kunye ne-base effects. I-core inflation ihlale ingaphezulu komphakathi woluhlu ekujoliswe kulo, ixhaswa luxinzelelo lwamaxabiso eenkonzo kunye nokudluliselwa kwemivuzo.

### Imarike yeMisebenzi

Iimpawu zemarike yemisebenzi zihlale zixubeneyo: ukungaphangeli kunyuke kancinci, kodwa ukuthatha inxaxheba kubonise ukuphucuka okuncinci. Ukukhula kwemivuzo kuqhubekile nokubeka uxinzelelo oluphezulu kwi-core inflation.

## Iimarike zeMali noLwelo

Amanqanaba emarike yemali alandele indlela ye-repo rate, ngelixa izivuno zeebhondi zikarhulumente zithambe kancinci njengoko uvakalelo lomngcipheko luphucukile. Ulwelo kwimarike ye-interbank luhlale lwanele, luxhaswa yimisebenzi ye-Contoso Bank xa kukho imfuneko.

## Amanyathelo oMgaqo-nkqubo neMbonakalo

IGunya loMgaqo-nkqubo weMali ligcine i-repo rate ingatshintshanga kuyo yonke ikota ukuze livavanye iimpembelelo ezilibazisekileyo zokuqiniswa kwangaphambili. Phambili, izigqibo zomgaqo-nkqubo ziza kulinganisa phakathi kokufezekisa injongo yokunyuka kwamaxabiso kunye nokuxhasa ukubuyela okuzinzileyo.

- Imingcipheko yexesha elifutshane: imfuno yangaphandle eguqukayo, ukothuka kwamaxabiso kwicala lonikezelo
- Ingqwalasela yomgaqo-nkqubo: ukubeka iliso kwi-core inflation, ukuqinisekisa ukusebenza kweemarike, nokunxibelelana ngokucacileyo
