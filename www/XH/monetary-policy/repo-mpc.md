# Repo and MPC

The policy committee sets the benchmark policy rate, often referred to as the repo rate in this sample. The repo rate guides short-term funding conditions and influences broader borrowing costs in the economy.

## Committee Process

1. Pre-briefing on inflation, activity, and financial conditions.
2. Roundtable discussion of policy options and scenarios.
3. Individual votes by members.
4. Publication of decision and minutes summary.

## What the Committee Considers

- Inflation level and persistence
- Inflation expectations from surveys and markets
- Domestic demand and labor-market pressures
- Exchange-rate pass-through and imported inflation
- Global financial conditions and commodity trends

## Transmission of Repo Changes

Repo-rate changes typically affect:

- Interbank rates first
- Prime and lending rates next
- Household and business financing conditions over time
- Inflation and output with variable lags

## Transparency Commitments

- Published policy calendar
- Timely decision statements
- Regular explanatory outreach for non-specialist audiences
