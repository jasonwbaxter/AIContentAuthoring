# Interest Rates

Interest-rate decisions are a central part of monetary policy. Changes in the policy rate affect borrowing and saving costs across households, firms, and government.

## How a Rate Decision Works

- The committee sets a policy-rate level.
- Money-market rates adjust first.
- Commercial banks reprice selected lending and deposit products.
- Spending, investment, and inflation adjust over time.

## Why Rate Changes Can Take Time

Monetary transmission is not immediate. Full effects on inflation and growth can take several quarters, so decisions are forward-looking and based on projected risks.

## Key Rate Indicators Published

- Policy rate (benchmark)
- Prime lending reference rate
- Short-term market reference rates
- Inflation and expectations context

## Public Guidance

We do not pre-commit to a fixed future path for rates. Guidance is conditional on incoming data, inflation risks, and financial conditions.
