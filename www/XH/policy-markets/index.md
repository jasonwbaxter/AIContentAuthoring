# Monetary Policy and Markets

This section explains how policy decisions are translated into market outcomes through operational tools, communication, and risk management.

## Why This Matters

Monetary policy affects funding costs, credit conditions, savings returns, and inflation dynamics. Clear implementation supports stable markets and credible policy transmission.

## Core Components

- Policy decision process (MPC)
- Repo and liquidity framework
- Market operations and collateral policy
- Interest-rate indicators and publication standards

## Implementation Principles

- Predictable operations with documented frameworks
- Equal treatment of eligible counterparties
- Risk-managed collateral and settlement controls
- Transparent communication during normal and stressed conditions
