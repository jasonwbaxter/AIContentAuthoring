# Repo and MPC

The Monetary Policy Committee sets the policy stance and repo benchmark used to guide short-term funding conditions.

## Meeting Framework

1. Data review and forecast briefing.
2. Scenario testing and policy alternatives.
3. Vote and final decision.
4. Statement publication and media briefing.

## Inputs to Decisions

- Inflation trajectory and risks
- Growth and labor-market indicators
- Exchange-rate and external-price dynamics
- Financial conditions and market functioning

## Communication Standards

- Publication at pre-announced times
- Clear explanation of the policy rationale
- Risk balance and forward-looking guidance
