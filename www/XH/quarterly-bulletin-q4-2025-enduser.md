# IBhulethini yeKota - Q4 2025

## Oko Kufuneka Ukwazi

I-Q4 2025 izise iindaba ezihle kancinane malunga nokunyuka kwamaxabiso, kuba sele kuqalile ukuthomalala. Nangona kunjalo, amaxabiso ezinto zemihla ngemihla ezifana neenkonzo asenyuka ngesantya esikhawulezayo kuneleyo i-Contoso Bank. Nantsi into eyenzekileyo nento ethetha yona kuwe.

## Amanani Aphambili kwiMali Yakho

- **Ukunyuka kwamaxabiso kuyathomalala:** Amaxabiso anyuke nge-5.1% xa kuthelekiswa nonyaka ophelileyo (kungcono kunangaphambili)
- **I-Core inflation (ngaphandle kokutya namandla):** Iseku-4.6%
- **Umvuzo wakho:** Imivuzo yokwenyani iqhubekile nokukhula, inceda ukumelana neendleko ezinyukayo
- **Ukungaphangeli:** Kuhleli kufutshane no-6.3%
- **Iindleko zokuboleka (i-Repo rate):** Zigcinwe ku-8.25%

> La manani asetyenziselwa umzekelo wokubonisa.

## Kwenzeka Ntoni kuQoqosho

Uqoqosho lwehlabathi luthambile kwi-Q4, kodwa ekhaya abantu baqhubekile nokuchitha ngenxa yokukhula kwemivuzo kunye nokuzinza kwemisebenzi. Utyalo-mali lwamashishini belungalingani, namanye amacandelo enza ngcono kunamanye.

### Kutheni Amaxabiso Esenyuka?

Ukunyuka kwamaxabiso kuthambile, ikakhulu ngenxa yokwehla kwamaxabiso amandla kunye ne-base effects ezilungileyo zonyaka ophelileyo. Kodwa iinkonzo ezifana nezempilo, imfundo, nokutya ngaphandle zisabiza ngakumbi. Iinkampani zidlulisela iindleko zemivuzo ephezulu kubathengi ngokunyusa amaxabiso.

### Umsebenzi noMvuzo Wakho

Imarike yemisebenzi ihlale izinzile ngokubanzi. Ukungaphangeli kunyuke kancinci, kodwa abantu abaninzi bangene kwimarike yemisebenzi. Ukukhula kwemivuzo kuqhubekile, kunceda abasebenzi bahambisane nokunyuka kwamaxabiso, nangona kungasoloko kuphelele.

## Kwenzeka Ntoni kwiimarike zeMali

Iibhanki kunye namaziko ezimali zinesixa semali esaneleyo. Amanqanaba enzala kwimarike yemali alandela i-repo rate esemthethweni. Iibhondi zikarhulumente ziye zaba nexabiso elingcono kancinci njengoko abatyalomali beziva benethemba ngakumbi.

## Yintoni Eyenziwa liGunya loMgaqo-nkqubo weMali

I-Contoso Bank igqibe ukugcina inqanaba lenzala lingatshintshanga ku-8.25%. Ifuna kuqala ukubona iimpembelelo zokunyuswa kwenzala okwedlulileyo ngaphambi kokwenza olunye utshintsho. Balungelelanisa iinjongo ezimbini:
- Ukubuyisela ukunyuka kwamaxabiso kwinqanaba elifunekayo
- Ukuqinisekisa ukuba uqoqosho luyakhula ngendlela ezinzileyo

**Izinto ekufuneka uzilandele:**
- Imfuno yehlabathi enokutshintsha kakhulu inokuvelisa iingxaki
- Ukothuka konikezelo (ukuphazamiseka kwimveliso) kunokunyusa amaxabiso
- I-Contoso Bank iya kuhlala ilandelela amaxabiso eenkonzo ngokusondeleyo

## Oko Kuthetha Kona Kuwe

**Iindaba ezihle:** Ukunyuka kwamaxabiso kuyancipha, kwaye imivuzo iqhubekile nokunyuka.

**Izinto zokujonga:** I-core inflation isengaphezulu kwe-target, kwaye ukungaqiniseki kwehlabathi kunokuchaphazela umsebenzi wakho okanye inkcitho yakho.

**Amanyathelo alandelayo:** I-Contoso Bank iya kuqhubeka ibeka iliso kumaxabiso kwaye ingahlengahlengisa amanqanaba enzala xa kuyimfuneko.
