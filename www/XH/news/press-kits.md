# Press Kits

Press kits provide media-ready background materials for major policy events and institutional announcements.

## Typical Contents

- Executive summary of the announcement
- Background note and technical appendix
- Speaker bios and institutional factsheet
- Approved visual assets and captions
- Q&A guidance for common media questions

## Distribution Rules

- Released at or after official publication time.
- Embargoed documents include explicit release conditions.
- Version updates are logged with timestamp and change summary.

## Access Support

If you need high-resolution assets, data tables, or broadcast-ready material, contact the media desk with your production specifications.
