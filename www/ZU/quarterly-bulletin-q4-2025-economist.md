# Ibhulethini Yekota - Q4 2025

## Isifinyezo Esiphezulu

I-Q4 2025 ibone ukuthamba kokwehla kwamandla emali ayinhloko kuya ku-5.1% (y/y) kanye nomsebenzi oqinile emikhakheni ebalulekile yomnotho, nakuba izimo zokukhula komhlaba zibe buthakathaka. Isiphathimandla Senqubomgomo Yezezimali sigcine isimo senqubomgomo ku-825 basis points kuyo yonke ikota, sisho ukuhlala kuqinile kwe-core inflation kanye nokungaqiniseki okuphezulu kwangaphandle. Le bhulethini inikeza ukuhlola okubanzi kwezinguquko ze-macro, izimo zezimakethe zezezimali, kanye nezinto ezicatshangelwayo zenqubomgomo kwikota yokugcina ka-2025.

## Amaphuzu Avelele

- **I-Headline CPI (y/y):** 5.1% (Q4 2025, yesikhashana)
- **I-Core CPI (y/y):** 4.6% (ngaphandle kokudla namandla, ngaphezu kwe-4.5% midpoint ehlosiwe)
- **I-Real GDP (q/q, annualised):** 1.2% (Q4 2025, yesikhashana) - yehlile isuka ku-1.5% ku-Q3
- **Izinga lokungasebenzi:** 6.3% (Q4 2025) - lenyuke lisuka ku-6.1% ku-Q3
- **I-Repo rate (ekupheleni kwesikhathi):** 825 bps (igcinwe kuyo yonke ikota)
- **Ukubamba iqhaza kwabasebenzi:** kuthuthuke kancane emaphethelweni

> Qaphela: Amanani ayidatha yesampula yesikhashana ngezinhloso zokubonisa. Ukukhishwa okusemthethweni kusalindile.

## Isimo Somnotho

Ukukhula komnotho womhlaba kubuthakathaka phakathi nekota, kubonisa isidingo sangaphandle esibuthakathaka kozakwethu ababalulekile bezohwebo kanye nokuqina kwezimo zezezimali zomhlaba. I-manufacturing PMI isahlezi ngaphansi kuka-50 emazweni amaningi athuthukile. Ekhaya, ukuqina kokusetshenziswa kwabathengi kusekelwe ukukhula kwamaholo nokuzinza kwemakethe yabasebenzi, kanti izinhloso zotshalomali zibe buthaka.

### Amanani Ne-Dynamics Yokwehla Kwamandla Emali

Ukuthamba kwe-headline inflation kusuka ku-5.6% (Q3 2025) kuya ku-5.1% (Q4 2025) kuqhutshwe ikakhulukazi yilezi:

1. **I-base effects** evela ekuguquguqukeni kwamanani amandla ku-Q4 2024
2. **Ukuncipha kwamanani amandla** ngemuva kokubuyekezwa kabusha kwamanani kawoyela womhlaba
3. **Ukuthamba kokwehla kwamandla emali kokudla** kuya emananini aphakathi

Nokho, ukuhlala kuqinile kwe-core inflation ku-4.6% kubonisa:

- **Ingcindezi yamanani ezinsizakalo:** ikakhulukazi kwezempilo, kwezemfundo, nasekuhlaleni
- **I-wage-price dynamics:** ukudluliselwa kwezindleko zabasebenzi kubahlinzeki bezinsizakalo
- **Ukulungiswa kwamanani alawulwayo:** amanani ezinsiza nezokuthutha
- **Amandla emananini emikhakheni:** angalingani phakathi kwezimpahla nezinsizakalo

### Dynamics Yemakethe Yabasebenzi

Imakethe yabasebenzi ibonise umfutho oxubile:

- **Ukungasebenzi kwenyuke** kusuka ku-6.1% kuya ku-6.3%, kukhombisa ukuthamba kwesidingo sabasebenzi
- **Ukubamba iqhaza kwabasebenzi kuthuthuke kancane**, kukhombisa ukushintsha okubuyela emuva komjikelezo
- **Ukuqhubeka kokukhula kwamaholo** kuhlale kuphezulu, kugcina ingcindezi yokwehla kwamandla emali
- **Umehluko wemikhakha:** ukukhula kwemisebenzi ezinsizakalweni kudlule emikhakheni ekhiqiza izimpahla

Ubudlelwano be-Phillips curve busabonakala, yize kukhona ukushintsha kwesakhiwo okuhlobene nezici ze-demography kanye nokungahambi kahle kwemakethe yabasebenzi.

## Izimakethe Zezezimali Nezimo Zoketshezi

### Izimakethe Zemali

Amazinga e-interbank overnight athengiselane eduze ne-repo rate engu-825 bps, okubonisa okulindelwe okunamathele kahle kwenqubomgomo. Imisebenzi ye-corridor igcine ukusebenza kahle ngokuguquguquka okuncane. Ukusetshenziswa kwe-standing facility kuhlale kusezingeni eliphansi, kubonisa uketshezi olwanele emakethe.

### Imakethe Yezibambiso Zikahulumeni

I-yield curve ibonise ukuthamba ekugcineni kwesikhathi eside, lapho izivuno zeminyaka eyi-10 zehle cishe ngo-40 basis points q/q. Lokhu kubuyekezwa kwamanani kubonisa:

- ukuthuthuka komuzwa wobungozi womhlaba ngemva kokungaqiniseki kwe-Q3
- okulindelwe ukuthi ukwesekwa kwenqubomgomo kuhlale isikhathi eside emhlabeni
- ukugeleza kwemali okuya ezibambisweni zikahulumeni zezimakethe ezisathuthuka

### Izimo Zemakethe Yezikweletu

Ukukhula kwezikweletu zamabhange kuthambile kuya ku-4.2% (y/y) kusuka ku-4.8% ku-Q3 2025, kubonisa isidingo esibuthakathaka ezigabeni zotshalomali, kodwa izikweletu zabathengi zihlale ziqinile. I-NPL ratio ihlale ku-3.1%.

## Izenzo Zenqubomgomo Yezezimali Nokuholwa Okubheke Phambili

Isiphathimandla Senqubomgomo Yezezimali sigcine izinga lenqubomgomo ku-825 basis points phakathi kwe-Q4 2025, sisho:

1. **Imiphumela yokudluliselwa ebambezeleke** yokusebenza kwangaphambilini okusadinga ukuhlolwa
2. **Ukuhlala kuqinile kwe-core inflation** ngaphezu kwebhendi ehlosiwe
3. **Ukungaqiniseki kwangaphandle** okudinga indlela eqaphelayo
4. **Uzinzo lwezezimali** kanye nezimo zezikweletu

### Ukuhlolwa Kwesimo Senqubomgomo

Isimo esingathathi hlangothi sibonisa ukuhlola okulinganiselwe:

| Isici | Ukuhlola | Isiqondiso |
|---|---|---|
| Umfutho we-inflation | Uyathamba kodwa i-core isanamandla | Kancane hawkish |
| Dynamics yokukhula | Iyathamba, ingaphansi komkhuba | Dovish |
| Izimo zezezimali | Ziyaqina emhlabeni | Dovish |
| Imakethe yabasebenzi | Iyaphola isuka ezingeni eliphansi | Neutral |

### Ukuholwa Okubheke Phambili

I-MPC izoqhubeka nokuhlola idatha ngesikhathi sangempela, igxile kulezi:

- indlela ye-core inflation namandla entengo ezinsizakalweni
- izinkomba zesidingo sangaphandle namanani ezimpahla
- izinkomba zozinzo lwezezimali zasekhaya
- izinkomba zokuqashwa ngesikhathi sangempela

Izinguquko zenqubomgomo zizoncika kudatha futhi zixhunywe nokuxhumana okucacile ngesikhathi esifanele.

## Ukuhlolwa Kwezingcuphe

### Izingozi Ezikhuphulayo Kokwehla Kwamandla Emali

1. **Ukushaqeka ohlangothini lokuhlinzeka:** ukungezwani kwe-geopolitics okuthinta amandla nokudla
2. **I-wage-price spirals:** ukukhula kwamaholo okuphezulu okuqhubekisela phambili i-inflation yezinsizakalo
3. **Ukumangala kwesidingo somhlaba:** ukujika kokuncipha kwe-inflation kozakwethu bezohwebo
4. **Ukwehla kwenani lemali:** i-pass-through ezindlekweni zokungenisa impahla

### Izingozi Ezehlisayo Ekukhuleni

1. **Ukuwa kwesidingo sangaphandle:** ukwehla komnotho kozakwethu ababalulekile bezohwebo
2. **Ukuqina kwezimo zezezimali:** ukuphuma kwemali nokwenyuka kwe-risk premia
3. **Ukuncipha kwezikweletu zasekhaya:** uma izimo zezezimali ziqina kakhulu
4. **Imiphumela yokuthembeka:** ukungaqiniseki ngenhloso yenqubomgomo okuvimbela utshalomali

## Isithasiselo: Amanothi Obuchwepheshe

### Imithombo Yedatha Nezindlela

- **Idatha ye-CPI:** iNational Statistics Authority, ukukhishwa kwanyanga zonke nokulungiswa kwesizini usebenzisa i-X-13ARIMA-SEATS
- **Izilinganiso ze-GDP:** indlela ye-flash estimate ehambisana nezinga le-SNA 2008
- **Idatha yabasebenzi:** iQuarterly Labour Force Survey enesisindo sabantu esilungisiwe
- **Amazinga emakethe yemali:** amanani esethenjwa abalwa njenge-volume-weighted daily average kusuka ezinkontilekeni ze-interbank

### Ukulungiswa Kwesizini

Wonke amasethi, ngaphandle kwezinga lokungasebenzi, alungiswe ngokwesizini. Ukulungiswa kwe-CPI kubonisa amaphethini esizini ezigabeni zokudla namandla.

### Isimo Sesikhashana

Amanani e-Q4 2025 asesimweni sesikhashana kuze kuthunyelwe idatha yokugcina yiziphathimandla zezibalo. Kulindeleke ukubuyekezwa kulezi:

- I-GDP (ukukhishwa kukaFebhuwari 2026)
- Idatha yokuqashwa (inani lokugcina ngoMeyi 2026)
- Izingxenye ezinemininingwane ze-CPI (ukuqinisekiswa kwedatha yokuqala)
