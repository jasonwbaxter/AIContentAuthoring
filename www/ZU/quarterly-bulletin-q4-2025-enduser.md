# Ibhulethini Yekota - Q4 2025

## Okudingeka Ukwazi

I-Q4 2025 ilethe izindaba ezinhle kancane mayelana nokwehla kwamandla emali, ngoba lokhu sekuthambile. Nokho, amanani ezinto zansuku zonke ezifana nezinsizakalo asakhula ngesivinini esiphezulu kunalokho i-Contoso Bank efuna ukukubona. Nansi into eyenzekile nokuthi kusho ukuthini kuwe.

## Izinombolo Ezibalulekile Kwisabelomali Sakho

- **Ukwehla kwamandla emali kuyathamba:** Amanani enyuke ngo-5.1% uma kuqhathaniswa nonyaka odlule (kungcono kunangaphambilini)
- **I-Core inflation (ngaphandle kokudla namandla):** Isese-4.6%
- **Iholo lakho:** Amaholo angempela aqhubekile nokukhula, esiza ukubhekana nezindleko ezikhuphukayo
- **Ukungasebenzi:** Kuhlale kusondele ku-6.3%
- **Izindleko zokuboleka (i-Repo rate):** Zigcinwe ku-8.25%

> Lezi zinombolo ziyisampula yokubonisa kuphela.

## Kwenzekani Emnothweni

Umnotho womhlaba uthambile ku-Q4, kodwa ekhaya abantu baqhubekile nokusebenzisa imali ngenxa yokukhula kwamaholo nokuzinza kwemisebenzi. Utshalomali lwamabhizinisi belungalingani, kanti eminye imikhakha yenze kangcono kuneminye.

### Kungani Amanani Esakhuphuka?

Ukwehla kwamandla emali kuthambile, ikakhulu ngenxa yokwehla kwamanani amandla kanye ne-base effects ezinhle zonyaka odlule. Kodwa izinsizakalo ezifana nezempilo, imfundo, nokudla ngaphandle zisabiza kakhulu. Izinkampani zidlulisela izindleko zamaholo aphezulu kubathengi ngokunyusa amanani.

### Umsebenzi Neholo Lakho

Imakethe yabasebenzi ihlale izinzile ngokujwayelekile. Ukungasebenzi kwenyuke kancane, kodwa abantu abaningi bangene emakethe yabasebenzi. Ukukhula kwamaholo kuqhubekile, kusiza abasebenzi ukuba bahambisane nokwehla kwamandla emali, yize kungahlali kuphelele.

## Kwenzekani Ezimakethe Zezezimali

Amabhange nezikhungo zezezimali zinemali eyanele. Amazinga enzalo emakethe yemali alandela i-repo rate esemthethweni. Amabhondi kahulumeni abe nesimo esingcono kancane njengoba abatshalizimali bezwa ukuzethemba okwengeziwe.

## Okwenziwa Isiphathimandla Senqubomgomo Yezezimali

I-Contoso Bank inqume ukugcina izinga lenzalo lingaguquki ku-8.25%. Ifuna kuqala ukubona imiphumela yokwenyuswa kwenzalo kwangaphambilini ngaphambi kokwenza izinguquko ezengeziwe. Ilinganisa izinhloso ezimbili:
- Ukubuyisela ukwehla kwamandla emali ezingeni elifanele
- Ukuqinisekisa ukuthi umnotho uyakhula ngendlela esimeme

**Okufanele ukubheke:**
- Isidingo somhlaba esingaqagelwanga singadala izinkinga
- Ukushaqeka kokuhlinzeka (ukuphazamiseka ekukhiqizeni) kungakhuphula amanani
- I-Contoso Bank izoqhubeka iqaphe amanani ezinsizakalo ngokusondele

## Isifinyezo Esisheshayo Sedatha Ebalulekile

| I-Core inflation (ngaphandle kokudla/mandla) | 5.2% | 4.9% | 4.6% |
| Ukukhula komnotho | 2.8% | 1.5% | 1.2% |

## Lokhu Kusho Ukuthini Kuwe

**Izindaba ezinhle:** Ukwehla kwamandla emali kuyancipha, kanti amaholo ayaqhubeka nokukhula.

**Okumele uqaphe:** I-core inflation isengaphezu kwe-target, futhi ukungaqiniseki komhlaba kungathinta umsebenzi wakho noma indlela osebenzisa ngayo imali.

**Izinyathelo ezilandelayo:** I-Contoso Bank izoqhubeka iqaphe amanani futhi ingalungisa izinga lenzalo uma kudingeka.
