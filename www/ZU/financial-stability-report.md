## Imiphumela Yokuhlolwa Kwengcindezi

### Ukuhlaziywa Kwezigameko

Senza ukuhlolwa kwengcindezi njalo ukuze sihlole ukuqina kohlelo lwezezimali:

#### Isigameko 1: Ukushaqeka Kwezinga Lenzalo
**Incazelo**: Ukwenyuka ngo-200 basis points esivunweni
**Umthelela**: Izilinganiso zenhloko-mali zehla ngamaphuzu angu-1.8
**Ukuhlola**: Uhlelo luhlala luqinile

#### Isigameko 2: Ukushaqeka Kwezikweletu
**Incazelo**: Ukwenyuka ngo-50% kwezinga lokwehluleka ukukhokha
**Umthelela**: Izilinganiso zenhloko-mali zehla ngamaphuzu angu-2.3
**Ukuhlola**: Uhlelo luhlala lunenhloko-mali eyanele

#### Isigameko 3: Ukwehla Kwemali
**Incazelo**: I-ZAR yehla ngo-15% uma iqhathaniswa nezimali ezinkulu
**Umthelela**: Ukuchayeka kwezinguquko zemali kuyakhula kodwa kuyaphatheka
**Ukuhlola**: Izingozi zilawulwa
