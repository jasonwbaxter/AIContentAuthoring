# Ibhulethini Yekota - Q4 2025

## Isifinyezo Esiphezulu

I-Q4 2025 ibonise ukuthamba kokwehla kwamandla emali ayinhloko ngesikhathi umsebenzi womnotho uqhubeka uqine emikhakheni ebalulekile. Isiphathimandla Senqubomgomo Yezezimali sigcine isimo senqubomgomo singaguquki kuyo yonke ikota, sisho ukuthi i-core inflation isanamandla kanti nezimo zangaphandle zisalokhu zingaqinisekile. Le bhulethini inikeza uhlolojikelele lwezinguquko ze-macro, izimo zezimakethe zezezimali, kanye nezinto ezicatshangelwayo zenqubomgomo ekupheleni kuka-2025.

## Amaphuzu Avelele

- I-Headline CPI (y/y): 5.1% (Q4 2025, inani lesampula)
- I-Core inflation (y/y): 4.6% (ikhishwe ukudla namandla)
- I-Real GDP (q/q, annualised): 1.2% (Q4 2025, yesikhashana)
- Izinga lokungasebenzi: 6.3% (Q4 2025)
- I-Repo rate: 8.25% (izinga lenqubomgomo ekupheleni kwekota)

> Qaphela: Amanani angenhla ayisampula yokubonisa kuphela.

## Isimo Somnotho

Ukukhula komnotho womhlaba kuthambile phakathi nekota, kubonisa isidingo sangaphandle esibuthakathaka kanye nezimo zezezimali zomhlaba eziqinile. Ekhaya, ukusetshenziswa kwabathengi kusekelwe ukukhula kwamaholo nokuzinza kwemakethe yabasebenzi, kanti utshalomali belungalingani phakathi kwemikhakha.

### Amanani Nokwehla Kwamandla Emali

Ukwehla kwamandla emali kuthambile ku-Q4, ngokwengxenye ngenxa yokwehla kwamanani amandla kanye ne-base effects. I-core inflation ihlale ingaphezu kwephuzu eliphakathi kwebhendi ehlosiwe, iqhutshwa ingcindezi yamanani ezinsizakalo kanye nokudluliselwa kwamaholo emananini.

### Imakethe Yabasebenzi

Izinkomba zemakethe yabasebenzi zihlale zixubene: ukungasebenzi kukhuphuke kancane, kodwa ukubamba iqhaza kwabasebenzi kubonise ukuthuthuka okuncane. Ukukhula kwamaholo kuqhubekile nokufaka ingcindezi ephezulu ku-core inflation.

## Izimakethe Zezezimali Noketshezi

Amazinga emakethe yemali alandele indlela ye-repo rate, kanti izivuno zamabhondi kahulumeni ziye zathamba kancane njengoba umuzwa wobungozi uthuthuka. Uketshezi emakethe ye-interbank luhlale lwanele, lusekelwa yimisebenzi ye-Contoso Bank lapho kudingeka khona.

## Izenzo Zenqubomgomo Nombono Olandelayo

Isiphathimandla Senqubomgomo Yezezimali sigcine i-repo rate ingaguquki kuyo yonke ikota ukuze sihlole imiphumela ebambezeleke yokwethulwa kwezinqubomgomo eziqinile zangaphambilini. Ngokubheka phambili, izinqumo zenqubomgomo zizolinganisa phakathi kokufeza umgomo wokwehla kwamandla emali nokusekela ukubuyela esimweni sokukhula esisimeme.

- Izingozi zesikhathi esifushane: isidingo sangaphandle esiguqukayo, ukushaqeka kwamanani ohlangothini lokuhlinzeka
- Ukugxila kwenqubomgomo: ukuqapha i-core inflation, ukuqinisekisa ukusebenza kwezimakethe, nokuxhumana ngokucacile
