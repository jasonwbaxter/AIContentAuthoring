# Market Operations

Market operations align short-term money-market conditions with the policy stance set by the committee.

## Operations in Scope

- Open market operations
- Standing lending and deposit facilities
- Liquidity fine-tuning around known market pressure points

## Operational Goals

- Keep overnight and short-term rates orderly.
- Reduce temporary funding stress.
- Preserve smooth payment and settlement flows.

## Collateral and Counterparty Controls

- Predefined eligibility standards
- Valuation haircuts and concentration limits
- Ongoing monitoring and compliance checks
