# Market Operations

Market operations implement the policy stance set by the committee. Operations teams manage liquidity and short-term money-market conditions so that market rates align with the policy objective.

## Operational Toolkit

- Main refinancing and liquidity-providing operations
- Fine-tuning operations around settlement and tax dates
- Standing facilities to cap excessive rate volatility
- Eligible collateral frameworks and risk haircuts

## Objectives

- Keep short-term rates close to the policy target.
- Support orderly market functioning in stressed periods.
- Limit undue volatility in core funding markets.

## Risk Controls

- Counterparty eligibility standards
- Collateral valuation and concentration limits
- Real-time monitoring of settlement and liquidity flows

## During Market Stress

When funding conditions tighten abruptly, operations can be scaled within mandate boundaries to preserve transmission of monetary policy while maintaining prudent risk standards.
