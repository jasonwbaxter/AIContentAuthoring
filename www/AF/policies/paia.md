# PAIA Access to Information

This page explains how to request records under the Promotion of Access to Information Act (PAIA).

## Your Right of Access

PAIA enables people to request access to records held by public and private bodies, subject to lawful grounds for refusal and procedural requirements.

## How to Submit a Request

1. Complete the prescribed access request form.
2. Clearly identify the record you need.
3. Provide contact details for correspondence.
4. Submit through the designated information office.

## Processing and Outcomes

- Requests are acknowledged and assessed against PAIA requirements.
- You will receive a decision, reasons where applicable, and fee details if relevant.
- Internal or external review pathways are explained when a request is refused.

## Good Practice Tips

- Be specific about dates, departments, and document types.
- State the preferred form of access (inspection, copy, electronic).
